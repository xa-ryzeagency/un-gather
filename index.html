Unknown Nations takes God's Word to unreached peoples through more than 6,000 indigenous missionaries, from 40 outposts in 65 countries. Local believers carry it where no outsider can go, often as a Treasure: an audio Bible in someone's own language.

Questions

What is Gather27?

A free, 24-hour global gathering of worship, prayer and repentance on March 5–6, 2027, led by Gather, founded by Jennie Allen, and streamed worldwide. Learn more at gather27.com.

What is a watch party?

Two or more people watching together: a living room, a small group, a church. Gather calls it a Gather Group.

Do I have to watch all 24 hours?

No. Watch for as long as you like. You can pause and catch up, and replays run until March 8.

When is Unknown Nations on?

We'll send hosts and everyone praying with us the time of our hour as soon as it's confirmed.

What is Unknown Nations' part?

We're one of the organizations featured during the broadcast, sharing the story of the persecuted church and the people who have never heard.
